# LiveHealthy: Vitals v1.1 — plan

Agreed with the owner on 2026-11-24, based on `reports/Vitals app upgrade and AdMob.md`.
Branch: `v1.1` (`main` stays identical to the live v1.0.0 until release). Version: 1.1.0+2.

## Scope
1. **AdMob banner** (done on the branch): one labelled adaptive banner on the history/chart
   screens only; consent first (UMP); non-personalized; test unit in debug builds.
2. **Statistics** on each history screen, for the selected 7/30/90 days: average, lowest,
   highest, number of readings, morning vs evening average (BP, pulse), share of readings in
   the normal range, glucose averages per context (fasting vs after meal).
3. **7-day BP check** (home monitoring protocol, ESH 2021 / AHA 2025): the average of the last
   7 days' readings, with the first day dropped, against the 135/85 home threshold. It states
   how many readings are still recommended when there are too few. Informational wording only.
4. **Doctor report**, free (the research says don't paywall it): PDF with patient, period,
   per-vital summary, chart and readings table, plus the disclaimer. Also a CSV of all
   readings. Both are shared through the Android share sheet (WhatsApp, email, …). No ads on
   the report screen. The PDF is in English for v1.1; Urdu PDF text rendering is unverified.

## Out of scope (v1.2+ candidates)
Remove-ads purchase, caregiver sharing, Medicine Reminder adherence on the BP chart, Health
Connect (needs Flutter 3.44+), widget, backup, tags, accessibility pass at 200%, voice/OCR/
Bluetooth. Avoid an "estimated HbA1c".
